Five-minute tour

  1. Watch it run. Trades and quotes flow; every RFQ gets a firm price in under a few milliseconds.
  2. Crash the leader. Press Crash on the LEADER. Read the timeline: new leader elected, trading resumed, trade count carries on.
  3. Bring it back. Start the dead member: it rejoins from its own log and catches up.
  4. Wipe a follower. It restarts with no state at all and rebuilds from the leader.
  5. Credit breach, then the kill switch. Watch the reject reasons appear. Undo the kill switch: a second user approves it (four-eyes).
  6. Burst the load. Take the rate to 200/s, then add 50ms delay to a follower and see what consensus costs.
